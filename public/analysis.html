1 · Survey data

Where this data lives The survey file you upload is held in this server's memory only. It is never saved to disk, never written to GitHub, never logged, and the page loads no outside scripts or services. It disappears when the server restarts or you press “Remove survey data”.

Drop the Qualtrics export (.csv) here, or choose it:

Qualtrics → Data & Analysis → Export Data → CSV (either “choice text” or “numeric values” works). The file needs a column holding each student's last 4 digits.

4 · Heat map

How to read it Each cell is a rank correlation (ρ, “rho”) between a survey variable (row) and agreement with a poll statement (column). Orange: students who score higher on the variable agree more. Blue: they agree less. Every correlation is shown, whether or not it is statistically significant. Markers: ** q < .05 · * q < .10 (both survive correction for the many comparisons) · † raw p < .05 only, which at this many comparisons is often chance. Click a cell for the breakdown; click a poll heading to sort by it.

Click a cell in the heat map to see how the poll answers break down.

5 · Class-safe view for the projector

The class view shows the poll histogram and the patterns that line up with it, but never a group smaller than 5 students, never an individual, and never a student code. Sensitive sections stay off unless you switch them on here.
